About me

I didn't come from computer science. I studied mechanical technology, and I think that's why I like taking websites apart to see what makes them slow.

Before the web, I spent over ten years in manufacturing: QA at onsemi, then maintaining the automated test equipment for semiconductor chips at Analog Devices. That work taught me to verify every fix myself instead of trusting a single score, and I still work that way.

In 2026 I went all in on web development: HTML and CSS first, then JavaScript, then React. I didn't want my learning to stay in tutorials, so I took a part-time web developer role with real client sites and built my own products alongside the courses.

Today I manage five live client sites, including a WooCommerce store: I build them, host them, migrate them, and handle their SEO. I write my own plugins when existing ones fall short, and ship Next.js apps to Vercel. Because SEO is half my job, I think about how a site gets found, not just how it looks.

Now I bring that same care to freelance work. If you need a new website, an online store, a faster site, or someone reliable to look after the one you already have, I would be glad to help.
